Demonstrating the catalogue-first proposition

HPDS WD 0.3 treats the pathway definition as the primary governed business artefact and the pathway catalogue as the means of discovery, comparison, reuse and lifecycle management. This demonstrator keeps the source catalogue intact, then exposes how its content maps into that model.

Immediate result

The existing 24-field catalogue is rich enough to seed pathway definitions, information requirements, activities, roles, stages, evidence links and implementation mappings. It does not by itself supply all HPDS minimum governance and clinical-authority fields.

No invented conformance. Missing purpose, owner, version, lifecycle status, authority status, intended use, explicit outcomes and decision-point declarations are shown as authoring gaps rather than inferred.

Why the separation matters

Fields that name UHSx sites, CareFlow, Miya, PAS, EPR capabilities and concrete interfaces are useful evidence about implementation. Under HPDS they do not define the pathway itself. The same business definition can therefore survive technology replacement and can be compared with implementations at other organisations.

Definition versus implementation

This view demonstrates the most important architectural boundary in HPDS. The pathway definition captures enduring clinical and business meaning. Named systems, sites, interfaces and local validation questions are attached as implementation material.

Conformance lab

HPDS WD 0.3 requires the minimum pathway definition dataset at every level. This is a source-to-standard completeness assessment, not a conformance declaration.

24-field catalogue to HPDS mapping

Catalogue anatomy

The demonstrator uses the seven clinical groups as catalogue classifications. They organise discovery without becoming the semantics of the pathway definitions themselves.

From spreadsheet row to governed business artefact

Source row + provenance→ Catalogue entry→ Pathway definition→ Structured elements & relationships→ Independent implementation mappings

At Level 1 the business artefact can remain intentionally coarse. Level 2 adds identified elements and complete structural and behavioural relationships. Level 3 adds semantic bindings. Level 4 adds computable criteria under a declared expression profile. Execution itself remains outside HPDS.
